Radio signals from 63 light-years away just made headlines. Is it an alien message? No — and the real story is wilder.

Astronomers using South Africa's MeerKAT telescope traced repeating radio bursts to the giant exoplanet Beta Pictoris b: a radio aurora powered by a magnetic field at least ~1,250 gauss — thousands of times stronger than Earth's. In this video: how the signal was pinned on the planet using nine quasars, what electron cyclotron maser emission is, why it isn't technology, and what comes next.
